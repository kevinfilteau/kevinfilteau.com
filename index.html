Work intelligently

AI can take routine, tedious tasks off your team — as long as you identify the right ones and know how to automate them.

Anyone can improvise with AI — that’s how it starts. To build something that lasts, draw on my experience: seeing how your systems, your team, and your constraints fit together, and working out what will hold up.

In practice, you equip your team. They learn to use artificial intelligence day to day, to recognize what’s worth automating, and to rely on software shaped around the way you work — not the other way around. The tedious work fades, and everyone can focus on what they’re really there for.

I like working with direct, curious people who think long term for their team.

“Let’s start with one real irritant, and look at what we can do together.”
