Work intelligently

AI can absorb your team’s repetitive, tedious work. The hard part is knowing which work — and how.

Anyone can improvise with AI — that’s how it starts. To build something that lasts, draw on my experience: seeing how your systems, your team, and your constraints fit together, and telling which choices will still hold up.

In practice, you equip your team: they learn to use AI day to day, to spot what’s worth automating, and to rely on software shaped around the way you work — not the other way around. The tedious work fades, and everyone gets back to what they do best.

“Taste can’t be improvised.”

I like working with calm, direct, and curious people who think long term for their team.

Write to me — start with one real irritant, and we’ll look at what we can do about it.
